Hardware Requirements

MorrowOS is designed to run on a wide range of systems. Here's what you'll need.

Minimum
  • RAM2 – 4 GB
  • Storage60 GB HDD+
  • Display720p-capable CPU/GPU
  • CPU Architecture32-bit (legacy build)
  • Integrated GraphicsIntel HD 520 / AMD Radeon Vega 3
  • FirmwareBIOS
Recommended
  • RAM8 GB+
  • Storage128 GB SSD+
  • Display1080p-capable CPU/GPU
  • CPU Architecture64-bit
  • GraphicsDedicated GPU recommended
  • FirmwareUEFI
